click.ogg derives from Audio/bong_001.ogg (about 0.072 seconds).
Changes: mono mix, playback pitch/rate raised by 1.7x, 120 Hz high-pass
(2nd-order Butterworth), 2400 Hz low-pass (3rd-order Butterworth),
3 ms attack and 18 ms release fades, peak normalized to 0.58.

complete.ogg derives from Audio/pluck_001.ogg (0.420 seconds).
Changes: mono mix, 100 Hz high-pass (2nd-order Butterworth), 1000 Hz
low-pass (4th-order Butterworth), 3 ms attack and 18 ms release fades.
The source A4 pluck is followed at 120 ms by a 1.5x-pitched E5 pluck;
relative gains are 0.78 and 1.0. Quiet copies at 55/100/160/220 ms use
gains 0.13/0.06/0.025/0.012 to soften the decay. The finished mix has
a 25 ms end fade and is peak-normalized to 0.72. No oscillator is added.

Both variations are Ogg Vorbis at the source sample rate. Processing uses
SciPy resample_poly / butter / sosfilt, sine-squared fade-in and
cosine-squared fade-out, and SoundFile Ogg Vorbis encoding.
